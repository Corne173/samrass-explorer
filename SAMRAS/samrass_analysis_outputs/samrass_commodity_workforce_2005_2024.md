# SAMRASS Commodity Workforce, 2005-2024

This file consolidates employment/workforce counts for the SAMRASS commodity groups available in the supplied Minerals Council workbook and PDFs. Counts are employee/workforce counts, not accident counts.

## Sources and Coverage

- 2005-2012 major commodity groups: `facts-and-figures-2012.pdf`, Table 6, "Labour history by commodity".
- 2013-2024 major Minerals Council sheets: `minerals_council_facts_and_figures_datasheets_-_2013_to_2024.xlsx`, commodity sheets, `Direct Employees (Number)` column.
- Additional smaller commodity timelines: `SAMI 2017-2018.pdf` and `SAMI 2022-2023.pdf` commodity employment tables.
- 2024 coverage is only available where the Excel workbook has a populated 2024 employee row. The supplied SAMI 2022-2023 PDF only extends to 2023.

Notes: `Industrial minerals` in the workbook is retained as its own aggregate group. Individual industrial-mineral tables from SAMI are also listed separately, so do not sum the aggregate and individual rows together. Blank cells mean no value was available in the supplied sources; `N/A` means the source explicitly reported `N/A`. Where SAMI 2017-2018 and SAMI 2022-2023 overlap, the newer SAMI 2022-2023 historical series is preferred unless it does not contain that year.
